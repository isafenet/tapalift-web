Who sees what

Who can see you, and when

What the people in your circles can see about you and your children. Tapalift never sees any of it.

Updated 17 October 2026

In short. In a Lifts circle (football, the school run, friends), people see your name and whether you're free for lifts. Not where you are, unless you choose to show them. A lift's pickup shows to the circle only roughly; the exact spot, and your photo and car, go only to the person in the lift with you. During a lift, only the two people in it see each other, until the pickup. In the Family circle, parents see the children, and a grown-up like Grandma only if a parent says so. Children always see who can see them.

In a Lifts circle

A Lifts circle is for anyone who isn't your immediate family: the football team, the school run, friends or work. People in it see your name (and your initials and colour, not a photo) and whether you're free for lifts right now. They don't see where you are unless you choose to show them: in the circle's settings, Where you are can show them roughly (to about a kilometre) or exactly, to the people you pick, paused when you like, and you always see who does. It's off unless you turn it on, under 16s can't, and at 16 and 17 your parents are told. They never see your home, your family's places, your battery or your journeys.

During a lift

When you ask for a lift, everyone you ask sees roughly where the pickup is (the area, like "Near Camden Town"), so they can decide if they can get there. Once someone says they'll go, they see the exact spot, and:

  • the rider sees the car coming, from "I'm on my way" until the pickup;
  • the driver sees where the rider is, so they can find them. For a lift later in the day, that starts half an hour before;
  • the rider sees the driver's photo, car and number plate: "Only get in if it's a blue Polo, AB12 CDE". They're sent from the driver's phone for that lift only, and forgotten when it ends;
  • each of them can call the other, if they've added their number;
  • for a child's lift (or someone with Care & Support), the driver gets a word for the pickup, like "Mango", and the child asks for it before getting in. Only the driver gets it, and it's forgotten at the pickup. (Older circles still moving from iCloud don't have it yet.)

Everyone else in the circle can see that the lift is happening, never where either person is. When the rider's picked up (or the lift is cancelled), the sharing stops and the positions are removed. Your iPhone shows its location sign the whole time.

This happens even if you've paused your sharing: it's how a lift works. You're told when it does.

After the pickup, on a child's lift outside the family, the child's own phone keeps an eye on the way, privately: nobody in the lift's circle sees anything. If the car goes well off the way, stops for 10 minutes, or is taking much longer than expected, the child's parents (or carers) are told and see where they are, and the child is asked "Is everything OK?". The way is worked out on the child's own phone and stays there.

An example: the school run

Say you drive your child and two friends, Mia and Leo, to school, as a planned trip in a School run circle that their families are in too.

  • The children see each other's names. Never each other's location, before, during or after the run.
  • Before pickup, from half an hour before, each child sees your car coming to them, until they're all in, and your car and number plate: "Only get in if it's Jay, in the blue Polo, AB12 CDE".
  • Phone numbers aren't shown on a trip at all: not yours, not the other parents'. If the organiser adds someone without Tapalift (a grandparent with no smartphone, say), their number goes only to the organiser and their driver, so the driver can ring them.
  • A child who chooses Share where I am shows only to you, the driver, until they're in the car. For a child under 13, your phone asks for Face ID first.
  • Pickups (usually each child's home) show only to that child's family, the driver and whoever organises the trip. Everyone else just sees "Pickup".
  • Once everyone's in the car, nothing is shared live in the circle.
  • The other parents see the plan (whose car their child is in, roughly when) and your ticks as you go: picked up, and home. On their own family map they see their own child, as always, never you or the other children. If their family's lift rules ask a parent first, they're asked "Can Jay give Mia a lift?", with your name, photo, car and number plate.

In the Family circle

  • Parents see the children on the family map: where they are, their journeys, their places, how they're travelling and their battery.
  • A grown-up in the family, like Grandma, sees the children only if a parent turns that on, and the children are shown that she can.
  • Brothers and sisters don't see each other, unless one chooses to let the others see them. It's their own choice, off unless they turn it on, and their parents are told.
  • Adults choose whether to share, and who in the family sees them, person by person.

Your children, at every age

How Tapalift knows your age. The first time Tapalift opens, it asks "How old are you?". If your iPhone shares an age range (Apple's Declared Age Range, which a parent sets on a child's Apple Account in Family Sharing), Tapalift uses that; otherwise you give your birthday. It stays on your phone, and only the age group counts. In a Family circle, the birthday a parent gives decides.

  • They always know. A child's phone always shows a sign that they're shared, and exactly who sees them, in line with the UK's Children's Code. When someone taps Check now, the person checked (child or adult) gets a quiet notification: "Sam checked where you are". It's at most once every half hour for each person, and never for the map just refreshing.
  • "Is this your family?" Before a child joins a Family circle, Tapalift asks them to make sure it really is their family. If they're not sure, or someone they met online asked them, it tells them not to join, and to talk to a grown-up they trust or call Childline.
  • Under 13. Children use an Apple Account set up through Apple's Family Sharing. Any phone that can see where they are needs Face ID (or the passcode) first, including the driver's phone on a lift in another circle. To start with, a parent is asked before every lift outside the family.
  • Under 16. They can't turn sharing off with their family, can't start circles, and join a circle outside the family only once a parent says yes (with no Family circle, they can only join one). They can't show other circles where they are, and can't stop sharing everywhere: the "Worried?" page helps them tell a grown-up instead. To start with, a parent is asked before a lift with a new driver.
  • 16 and 17. They can pause sharing for up to 3 hours at a time, and the family is told. They can join other circles, and choose to show them where they are; their parents are told. To start with, their parents are told about their lifts rather than asked.
  • 18. They become an ordinary member who chooses what to share, and their date of birth is removed.
  • Parents decide the rest. A parent can change when they're asked before a lift, and choose who can pick a child up, person by person: Always OK, Ask me every time, or Not allowed (never asked). Parents see who's in each of their child's circles and their lifts there; the child sees that too.
  • In other circles (say, football), a child shows as their name and initials only: never their location, home or school. Their lift gets a word for the pickup, and their own phone watches the way after the pickup.
  • Blocking. Children can't block anyone in the Family circle, and nobody can block a child there: a child talks to a parent instead. When a child blocks someone in another circle, their parents are told.
  • Curfews and battery. A parent can set a curfew: if they're not home by then, they and their parents are told, with where they were last seen. A low battery (20%, then 10%) tells them and their parents, with places nearby where they might charge.

Blocking someone

Tap someone in a circle's people (or … on a lift with them) and choose Block. They can't ask you for lifts, "Anyone free" never asks them for you, you don't see their lift requests or their photo and car, and they don't see where you are. They aren't told, but they may notice: the block is saved with your details in that circle, so every phone there can keep you apart. A lift already on its way carries on, so nobody is left stranded. Unblock any time in You › Support › Blocked people.

Help

Hold for Help and your family sees exactly where you are for the next hour, even if you'd stopped sharing. If you don't have a Family circle, everyone in your circles is told where you are.

Worried about who knows where you are?

If you're in danger now, call 999. Otherwise, in the order it helps (it's in Tapalift too, in You):

  • Stop sharing everywhere, quietly. Adults can stop sharing in every circle at once from this page in Tapalift. Nobody is told; they see your location is off, but not when or why. It also shows who can see you now, circle by circle, and lets you hide from one person. At 16 and 17 you can pause for up to 3 hours. Under 16, tell a parent or another adult you trust.
  • Block them. They can't ask you for lifts or see where you are (see Blocking someone).
  • Tell whoever runs the circle. The people who can let others into it can take anyone out straight away.
  • Leave the circle. You're taken out and stop sharing with everyone in it. Then you can delete Tapalift if you want to; deleting it on its own stops new updates, but the circle may still show where you last were, so leave first. Delete my Tapalift data (You › Support) does both: it takes you out of every circle and deletes what Tapalift keeps about you.
  • Tell us with Report a Concern: we reply within 24 hours.

Free, confidential help in the UK: Childline 0800 1111 (under 19s, 24 hours), National Domestic Abuse Helpline 0808 2000 247 (24 hours), Men's Advice Line 0808 801 0327, Samaritans 116 123 (24 hours).

What Tapalift sees

Nothing. Your circle is kept on Tapalift's own service, end-to-end encrypted, and only your circle's phones hold the keys. The service knows which phone wrote each locked record, so only that phone (or a phone that can let people into the circle) can change or delete it, but it can't read what's inside. Your photo and car stay on your own phone. Your phone number is stored with your settings so your circle's phones can show it at the right moment; it's locked, so nobody outside your circle can open it, including us. The privacy policy has the full detail.

Questions

Can people in my football circle see where I am?

Not unless you choose. In a Lifts circle nobody sees your location unless you turn on Where you are (roughly or exactly, and you pick who), or you're in a lift together: then only the two of you see each other, until the pickup. Under 16s can't turn it on.

Can they see my home address?

No. Your home and your family's places stay in the Family circle. The one exception is if you choose Home as the pickup for a lift you ask for: then that circle sees roughly where (the area, to about a kilometre), and only whoever says yes sees the exact spot. You can pick a spot down the road instead.

Who sees my car and number plate?

Only the person you're picking up, once you've said you'll go, so they can check it's you before they get in. On a planned trip, the riders in your car see it from half an hour before their pickup.

On the school run, do the other parents get my phone number?

No. Phone numbers aren't shown on a planned trip. Your number only shows to someone in a lift with you, and to your own family.

Who sees my phone number?

Tapalift shows it to the person in a lift with you, so you can call each other, and to your family on your card on the family map. It's kept with your settings, locked so only your circles' phones can open it.

On the school run, can the other children see where my child lives?

No. A pickup shows only to that child's family, the driver and whoever organises the trip. The other children and their parents just see "Pickup".

Can my child's football coach see where my child is?

No. A child in another circle shows there as their name and initials only. If the coach gives them a lift, the coach sees where they are from setting off until the pickup (on Tapalift's service), and only after Face ID if your child is under 13. Under 16, your child can only join the club's circle once you've said yes.

If I block someone, can they still see me?

No. Someone you block can't ask you for lifts and doesn't see where you are, and you don't see their lift requests or photo. They aren't told, but they may notice, and the block is saved in the circle so every phone there can keep you apart.

Can Tapalift see any of this?

No. Everything is locked in your circle on Tapalift's service (end-to-end encrypted), and only the phones in your circle have the keys. We don't have an account for you, and we never sell or share anything.
