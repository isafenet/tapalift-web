Support

How can we help?

Write to info@isafenet.app. We usually reply within two working days. The user guide (in the app: You › User Guide) covers everything step by step.

Getting started

  • Start a circle: open Tapalift, choose Family or Lifts, give it a name and your name, and say whether you give lifts. It's kept on Tapalift's service, end-to-end encrypted, so only your circle's phones can read it.
  • Invite someone: circle menu on Home › Invite Someone. Send the link any way you like, or show the QR code. When they open it, you let them in.
  • Join a circle: tap the invite link you were sent, then give your name.
  • Just looking? Tap Try a demo circle on the first screen. It's pretend, on your phone only.

Lifts

  • Ask: tap Tap for a lift, or shake your phone twice (you get 10 seconds to stop it), ask Siri, or use your Apple Watch. Tap a name to ask one person, or Ask for a lift later.
  • Give: answer I'm on my way, or tap More for "In 15 minutes", "Can't right now" or a lift for later. Directions open Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze.
  • When you're free: You › When you can give lifts, with a daily reminder if you like.

Common questions

How do I invite someone?

Open the circle menu on Home, then Invite Someone, and choose who it's for. Send the link by Messages or WhatsApp, or if they're with you, show the QR code and they scan it with their camera. Each invite works once, for one person, and stops after a day. When they open it, you let them in with one tap.

I'm not getting notifications

Check You › Notification settings in Tapalift, and your Focus modes. Alerts that matter (Help, a late or stopped journey) are Time Sensitive, so allow Time Sensitive notifications for Tapalift.

How do I block or report someone?

Tap them in the circle's people (or use … on a lift with them) and choose Block. They can't ask you for lifts or see where you are, and they aren't told. Unblock in You › Support › Blocked people. To report someone, a photo or anything else, use You › Support › Report a concern. A report is an email to info@isafenet.app from your own email, so you see everything it sends, and we reply within 24 hours. If someone is in danger now, call the emergency services.

How do I delete my Tapalift data?

You › Support › Delete my Tapalift data. It takes you out of every circle, deletes what Tapalift keeps about this phone on our service, then deletes everything Tapalift kept on the phone, as if it were new. If a circle needs you to choose first (say, you're the only parent who can let people into your family), it asks. Under 16, a parent takes your phone out of the family instead.

The screen is hard to read

Turn on You › Look › Easier to see: darker words, clearer edges, solid panels and bigger text, on this phone only. It also comes on by itself with the iPhone's Increase Contrast or Reduce Transparency. Bigger text in the iPhone's Settings works too.

My phone asks for a lift when I don't mean it to

Shaking twice starts a 10-second countdown, so you can stop it. To turn shaking off, go to You › Circle settings › Shake to ask for a lift.

The pickup was in the wrong place

Location can be off indoors. Move the pin, or search for a place, before you send.

My child's location isn't updating

On your child's phone, check Settings › Tapalift › Location is set to Always with Precise Location on, and that Background App Refresh and Motion & Fitness are on. Low Power Mode and Battery Saver update less often. If their phone has been still for a while, Check now asks it straight away.

Tapalift says it's using my location with the app closed

That's the Family circle, if you're a child in it or a parent who shares. Tapalift uses GPS only while you're moving, and closely only when it matters. Turn on Battery Saver in You, or stop sharing for a while in You › Your location. Outside a Family circle, your location is only used for lifts.

A watched journey didn't tick a stop

A stop needs to be big enough for a phone's reading on a pavement: remove it from the journey and add it again with a bigger Size. "On the bus" needs a stop with "bus" or "station" in its name; anything else says "in a car".

Someone on a trip doesn't have an iPhone

Add them to the trip, then tap Send them a link. They answer in their web browser on any phone, and the answer comes back to you as a text: tap the link in it, or copy the text and tap Paste under People without Tapalift. No smartphone? Tap their name under Answers and answer for them.

The guest's answer link didn't open Tapalift

Open it on the iPhone of whoever sent the invite (a coordinator), with Tapalift installed. If it opens in Safari, tap Open in Tapalift on the page, or copy the text and use Paste on the trip.

A lift wasn't asked in our other circle

Lifts are asked in another circle only for people in both circles, so invite the children to that circle too. It happens when everyone says no, nobody's free, or nobody answers in 10 minutes (an hour before, for a lift later).

How do I restore Tapalift Plus on a new phone?

You › Tapalift Plus › Restore, signed in with the same Apple Account. To cancel, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Does it work on Android?

Not yet: asking for and giving lifts needs an iPhone. Anyone can answer a planned trip from a link, on any phone.

Is Tapalift an emergency service?

No. Help alerts your family. If you're in danger, call 999 (or your local emergency number).

Your information

We can't see your circles, so we can't look anything up for you, and we'll never ask for your Apple Account password. To delete everything, use You › Support › Delete my Tapalift data. See the privacy policy for what Tapalift stores and exactly what that deletes, and who sees what.
