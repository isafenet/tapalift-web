1. Acceptance

By downloading, installing or using Tapalift, or answering a trip from a Tapalift link, you agree to these Terms of Use. If you don't agree, don't use it.

2. The service

Tapalift is an app for iPhone and Apple Watch, made by iSafeNet, for asking the people in your circles for lifts and giving them, sharing where you are with them, and planning trips together. Circles are stored in their owners' iCloud accounts and shared through iCloud. How Tapalift handles information is in the Privacy Policy.

3. Lifts are between you and your people

Tapalift passes messages between people who invite each other. It doesn't provide transport, employ or check drivers, or arrange payment. Whether to ask, offer or accept a lift, and everything about the journey (including the driver's licence, insurance, the car's roadworthiness, child car seats and the law on carrying passengers), is up to the people involved. Don't use Tapalift to offer lifts for payment that would need a licence.

Directions, pickup orders, times and arrival estimates are guides worked out from map data, and can be wrong. Drive safely and follow the road, the law and the signs, not the app. Don't use Tapalift while driving except in ways your local law allows.

4. Safety, and emergencies

Tapalift isn't an emergency service. Help alerts the people in your Family circle; it doesn't contact the emergency services, and alerts depend on phones, networks and Apple's services working. If you're in danger, call 999 (or your local emergency number). Location, watched journeys and alerts help a family keep in touch; they don't replace supervising children appropriately, and can be late or missed if a phone is off, out of signal, out of battery, or has location switched off.

5. Location and the Family circle

Only share someone's location with their knowledge and, where needed, their permission. Only add a child to a Family circle if you're their parent or carer, or have their parent's or carer's agreement. Don't use Tapalift to follow anyone without their knowledge.

6. Who can use Tapalift

Anyone with an Apple Account can use Tapalift. Children should use it in a circle set up by a parent or carer, who decides who's in it. Under 13s use Apple Accounts managed through Apple's Family Sharing.
